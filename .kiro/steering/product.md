# CommonGround: product

CommonGround is an empathetic AI parenting companion for people across the parenting journey: preparing for
parenthood, pregnancy and infancy, early childhood, school-age children, adolescence, and adult family relationships.

## Audience
Parents, parents-to-be, and adults navigating family relationships. Many arrive stressed, tired or unsure.
Assume no technical or clinical background.

## What CommonGround is (and is not)
It helps parents and teens understand possible perspectives, reflect on hard situations, and prepare for honest
conversations with each other. It is NOT a replacement for those conversations, for therapy, or for professional
support, and it never claims to know what another family member thinks or feels.

## Experience principles
- The chat is the primary surface. Profile notes are optional, secondary, and one click away.
- Parent view: calm, editorial; focused on possible perspectives (without assuming causes), intentions, preparing
  conversations, how a message might land, and general guidance.
- Teen view: bold and expressive; focused on putting feelings into words, interests, preparing for hard talks,
  deciding what to share, and general support with no pressure to disclose.

## Goals (current milestone)
- Offer warm, practical, general parenting support through a simple chat.
- Let the user optionally say which stage they are in so answers fit better.

## Principles
- Nonjudgmental and empathetic. Never shame a parent.
- Honest about limits: general support, not a clinician, therapist or emergency service.
- Plain language. Short, actionable suggestions over lectures.
- Calm, uncluttered UI. No dark patterns, fake urgency, fake testimonials, invented credentials or
  claims of effectiveness.
- Minimal data: no accounts, no database, nothing stored beyond the current browser session.

## Not built yet (do not imply otherwise in UI, copy or docs)
Retrieval-augmented generation, citations, evidence grounding, clinical or expert evaluation, accounts,
persistence, hosted LLM provider, deployment.
