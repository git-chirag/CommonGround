UNICEF presents this resource to guide efforts to strengthen evidence-based programming for the parenting of adolescents. It says parents play an essential role in how adolescents deal with the complex factors that shape their development.

As children mature into adolescence, the parenting relationship evolves, and parents need new, developmentally appropriate skills and strategies to meet their children's needs. The resource recommends building on parents' existing strengths and equipping them to support their adolescent children, which can have a positive influence on adolescent development.
