UNICEF India suggests that adolescents need to feel heard, not only corrected. Listening calmly, without rushing to judge or fix everything, can make difficult moments easier.

It notes that adolescents can be strongly affected by constant pressure, including pressure about behaviour, marks, appearance or future success, and encourages parents to let them know their worth is more than achievements.

The page recommends handling disagreements and arguments without aggression, using respectful communication, and staying calm when emotions run high: slowing down, reducing conflict, and helping the young person feel safe before trying to solve the problem.

It also advises respecting growing independence by offering guidance while allowing age-appropriate choices, privacy and responsibility, and being a safe person a teen can come to with worries, mistakes or relationship problems.
