Executive function is a group of skills that lets people focus, hold and work with information in mind, filter distractions, switch between tasks and revise plans. The Center compares it to an air traffic control system managing many flights at once.

This InBrief explains how these lifelong skills develop, what can disrupt their development, and how supporting them pays off in school and life.
